About TechPhase

Ghana's Premier IT Solutions Company

TechPhase Solutions was founded in 2014. We supply computers and accessories, various office consumables, as well as networking and system maintenance services for a wide range of organizations across Ghana.

We have an excellent track record of successful projects delivered in demanding environments. TechPhase combines best-of-breed technologies to build solutions driven by the ambition to make our clients more successful and help them accomplish their strategic goals.

We serve corporate customers, Government agencies, Educational institutions, and private consumers.

Our Mission

Deliver maximum value by supplying quality, affordable, and durable IT products with exceptional after-sales service and networking support.

Our Vision

Become the market leader in computer hardware, office supplies, and networking solutions in Ghana and beyond West Africa.
